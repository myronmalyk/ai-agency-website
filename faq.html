Frequently Asked Questions

Everything mortgage brokers ask before working with us.

How is this different from buying Floify or another mortgage AI tool? +

Off-the-shelf tools handle the common 70% of the work. We build the 30% that's specific to your business — your lender preferences, your file flow, your referral relationships. We often build on top of Floify or your CRM, not instead of them.

What kind of AI do you actually build? +

Custom automations (workflows that run lead intake, document collection, status updates, rate sheets, renewal tracking) and AI agents (24/7 lead qualifiers, document collectors, realtor follow-up agents). Built on top of whatever stack you already have — Floify, BluMortgage, Finmo, custom CRMs.

What does a typical engagement look like? +

Free 30-minute strategy call first. If there's a fit, a one-page scope with a flat fee. Most builds run 2–6 weeks. Optional monthly retainer after launch for ongoing work.

How fast can we start? +

Strategy call usually booked within a week. If there's a fit, most builds are scoped within a few days and deployed in 2–6 weeks.

Do you work with my LOS, CRM, or lender portal? +

Yes. I build on top of whatever you've got — Finmo, BluMortgage, Velocity, Floify, Carly, custom CRMs. If your stack is unusual, bring it up on the strategy call. If it has an API or a webhook, there's usually a way in.

Why mortgage brokers specifically? +

Because depth beats breadth. I've focused on this niche so I'm not learning your business on your dime. I know your lender list, your compliance constraints, and the workflows that actually matter for moving a file.

How is this different from hiring a virtual assistant? +

A VA is a good option for some workflows. The difference: an automation that runs 24/7 doesn't take vacation, doesn't quit, and costs less after month two. Often the right answer is a mix — automate the repetitive admin, keep the VA for the judgment-required work.

What does a typical engagement cost? +

Build engagements typically run $3K–$12K depending on scope. Optional retainers run $750–$2K/month. We scope after the free strategy call so the number is grounded in what you actually need, not a price list.

Are you BCFSA-registered? +

I'm an AI consultant, not a licensed mortgage brokerage, so BCFSA registration doesn't apply to me. I build workflows that respect BCFSA expectations — record-keeping, disclosure timelines, document retention — when relevant.

What happens after the strategy call? +

You get a written summary of what I'd recommend. If you want to engage me, I send a flat-fee scope. If you don't, you keep the strategy notes and run with them yourself. No obligation.
